# nvidia-ohne-huang: NVIDIA ohne Jensen Huang

> News short, made 2026-11-12, one day after the announcement. Publish within 7 days
> (flow L rule 5 applies to any news short), ideally 13 or 14 November.

## The idea in one sentence

After watching, the viewer can say: "Der Gründer von NVIDIA ist weg, aber einer, der
seit dem ersten Jahr 1993 dabei ist, sitzt jetzt ganz oben im Verwaltungsrat."

The question the hook asks (and the payoff answers): Was bleibt von der Firma, die an
einem Tisch bei Denny's begann, wenn ihr Gründer nach 33 Jahren geht?

## Flow

- **Chosen:** R, one small moment then pull out. The moment is the Denny's booth in
  1993; the pull-out is the company today, without its founder.
- **Rejected shape:** L, news explainer (event → why → root causes). It would have to
  explain *why* Huang left, and the only "why" available is unconfirmed media reports
  about his private life and an alleged espionage link. Stating them is a legal and
  factual risk and fights the channel's positive tone (§1). The release gives no reason
  (f-0006), so the video says exactly that.
- What replaces spoken numbering: the years, 1993 and 2026, on frame.
- The moment: three engineers at a Denny's in San José, 1993, about $40,000 (f-0011,
  f-0012).
